ABOUT THE WEBSITE

Preserving the Stories Behind Our Heritage

India is home to a remarkable collection of historical monuments, cultural traditions, festivals, art forms, and architectural wonders. Each reflects the creativity, knowledge, and experiences of generations.

Heritage & Culture is a website created to introduce visitors to these diverse aspects of India. It brings together information about important landmarks, celebrations, and traditional art forms in one place.

Through this project, we aim to encourage curiosity, cultural awareness, and appreciation for India's rich heritage.
